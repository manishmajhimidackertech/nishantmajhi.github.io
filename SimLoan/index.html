About Calculation

SimLoan uses the standard Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) formula:

EMI = P * r * (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1)

Where,
P = principal,
r = monthly interest rate (annual ÷ 12 ÷ 100),
n = number of months.
